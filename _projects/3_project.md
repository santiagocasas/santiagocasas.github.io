A graph-oriented approach to metadata crosswalk extraction and harmonization, designed to represent
mappings, schema fragments, and alignment evidence in a transparent and reusable form.

The project is part of work at [DLR](https://www.dlr.de) within the
[Helmholtz Metadata Collaboration (HMC)](https://helmholtz-metadaten.de/), focusing on making
heterogeneous metadata more explicit, inspectable, and operational at scale.

- [Repository (Helmholtz GitLab)](https://codebase.helmholtz.cloud/santiago.casascastro/MetadataMappingSssOM)
