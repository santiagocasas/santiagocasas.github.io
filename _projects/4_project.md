Interactive graph visualization of metadata infrastructure across Helmholtz centers, showing entities,
relationships, and schema structure. The project helps make semantic links, alignment candidates, and
crosswalk structures easier to inspect visually during analysis and communication.

Part of work at [DLR](https://www.dlr.de) on knowledge graph representations for scientific metadata
within the [Helmholtz Metadata Collaboration (HMC)](https://helmholtz-metadaten.de/).

- [Repository (Helmholtz GitLab)](https://codebase.helmholtz.cloud/santiago.casascastro/metadatagraph)
